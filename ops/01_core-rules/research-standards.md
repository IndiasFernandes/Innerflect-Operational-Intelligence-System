# Research Standards

## Evidence

- **Cite sources** with title, publisher, date retrieved, and URL when public.
- Prefer **primary** sources (official docs, filings, product pages) over unverified summaries.
- Separate **facts** from **interpretation**—label inference explicitly.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Maintain an **Assumptions** section listing what you had to guess.
- Maintain **Open questions** with suggested next steps to resolve them.

## Confidence

- Mark claims with **high / medium / low** confidence when uncertainty matters to decisions.
- Do not present thin evidence as certainty.

## Comparisons

- Compare competitors on **the same dimensions** (pricing motion, ICP, channel, proof, onboarding).
- Avoid strawmen—describe the strongest version of an alternative before critiquing.
