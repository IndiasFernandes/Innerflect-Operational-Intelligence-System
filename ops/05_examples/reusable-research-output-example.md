# Example: Reusable research output (fictional)

Aligned with [research report template](../03_templates/research-report-template.md). **Not** real market data.

## Question

Should Innerflect prioritize mid-market teams (50–200) vs solo operators for Q3 GTM?

## Executive summary

- Mid-market shows **higher expansion potential** in our anecdotal pipeline but **longer sales cycles**.
- Solo segment has **faster closes** but **higher churn** in early cohorts (small n).
- **Recommendation**: dual track—solo for velocity learning; mid-market for 2 named account experiments with success criteria.

**Confidence**: Medium (limited primary data).

## Scope

Included: last 9 months pipeline notes (anonymized), 12 support themes, 3 competitor pricing pages.

Excluded: Paid survey, formal win/loss analysis (not yet run).

## Key findings

### Finding 1

- Claim: Mid-market deals reference **team workflows** and **permissions** more often than solo deals.
- Evidence: 8/11 mid-market discovery call summaries (internal CRM excerpts, redacted).
- Confidence: Medium

### Finding 2

- Claim: Solo users churn spikes after day-30 if onboarding checklist incomplete.
- Evidence: Product analytics cohort (n=43) — TODO: validate with data team export.
- Confidence: Low

## Sources

| Source | Type | Date retrieved | Notes |
|--------|------|----------------|-------|
| Internal CRM summaries | Primary (internal) | 2025-03-01 | Redacted; not customer-identifiable |
| Competitor A pricing page | Primary (public) | 2025-02-28 | URL omitted in this example |

## Analysis

Segment choice is not purely ACV—**support capacity** and **roadmap fit** constrain mid-market promises today.

## Recommendations

1. Publish **ICP one-pager** with explicit who-not-for.
2. Run **two mid-market pilots** with written success metrics before broad outbound.

## Assumptions

- Pipeline notes are representative of true demand (may be referral-skewed).

## Open questions

- What is **acceptable CAC** per segment given current runway?
- Do we have **SSO** requirements blocking mid-market?
