# Communication Standards

## Principles

- **Lead with the decision or ask**—respect the reader’s time.
- **Specific and bounded**: timeframe, owner, next step, and definition of done.
- **Calm tone**—direct without blame; focus on systems and next actions.

## Writing

- Prefer **bullet lists** for multi-item updates; use prose for narrative context.
- **Link artifacts** (docs, tickets, PRs) instead of pasting large blocks unless needed offline.

## Client-facing

- Align commitments with **scope** and **proposal** artifacts; escalate ambiguity early.
- Never share **secrets** or **personal data** in email when a secure channel exists.

## Meetings and async

- End meetings with **owners** and **due dates** captured in writing.
- Default public or team channels for **durable decisions** so they are searchable.
