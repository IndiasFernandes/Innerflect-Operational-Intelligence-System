# Design Standards

## Principles

- **Clarity before decoration**—reduce cognitive load; prioritize scannable hierarchy.
- **Consistency** across components, spacing, typography, and tone for a coherent product surface.
- **Accessibility awareness**: sufficient contrast, focus states, semantic structure; deepen with project-specific targets (WCAG level) when defined.

## Visual system

- Use **design tokens or shared variables** when the codebase provides them.
- Avoid one-off hex values and magic numbers where a token exists.

## Content on interfaces

- **Action-oriented labels**; avoid jargon unless the audience expects it.
- **Error messages** explain what happened and what to do next.

## Brand alignment

For marketing surfaces, align with branding playbooks and [tone-of-voice](../02_playbooks/branding/tone-of-voice.md) decisions.
