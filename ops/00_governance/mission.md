# Mission

## What Innerflect Ops is

**Innerflect Ops** is Innerflect’s **Markdown-first operational intelligence system**: a single place for playbooks, templates, checklists, standards, and examples that teams and AI tools can reuse with confidence.

## What success looks like

- A teammate can **find the right procedure in minutes**, not by searching chat history.
- Outputs are **comparable over time** because templates and playbooks encode structure.
- Changes are **versioned**, **reviewed**, and **traceable**—not scattered across documents and prompts.
- AI-assisted work **defaults to reuse** of existing playbooks instead of inventing parallel instructions.

## What this repository is not

- Not a static “prompt library” with thousands of one-off strings.
- Not a substitute for judgment, client specifics, or legal review where required.
- Not a place for secrets (credentials, private client data). Use links to secure systems instead.

## Principles

1. **Operating logic over categories** — organize by outcome and workflow, not by tool names.
2. **One canonical home** — playbooks live in `ops/02_playbooks/`; `docs/` explains and links.
3. **Calm defaults** — prefer clarity and restraint over volume.
